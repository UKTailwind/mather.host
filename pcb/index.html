Pico Computer 3

The RP2350B home computer: HDMI, a USB hub, 8 MB PSRAM, a PCM5102 audio DAC, a DS3231 clock and an SD card. Three revisions are published:

  • V3.1: first release, with a CH334U USB hub
  • V3.2: the QFN USB-host power switch replaced by a SOT23-6 part
  • V3.3: a 1.3 V regulator with a better thermal connection to the board
