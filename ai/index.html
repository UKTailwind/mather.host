Local language models

Which AI models can you run on your own hardware that are actually useful for embedded C and MMBasic work? Ten models were tested on a workstation with a 20 GB graphics card, on real tasks: writing C that is checked under AddressSanitizer, answering questions about the MMBasic source, and extending a BASIC interpreter.

The write-up covers the method, the results, how to set up Ollama, and what more money buys, from a used 24 GB card to a 128 GB Ryzen AI Max+ machine.

Read the write-up

In short: gpt-oss:20b is the best all-rounder for a 16–24 GB card, and gemma4:26b, which fits a 24 GB card, matched the much larger gpt-oss:120b on real changes to existing code.
