Pico Computer 3

A complete small computer built around the RP2350B: HDMI output, a USB hub for keyboard, mouse and drives, 8 MB of PSRAM, stereo audio, a real-time clock and an SD card. It boots into MMBasic, MicroPython or the FUZIX Unix-like OS, and it can also act as a VT100 terminal.

About

Peter Mather has spent more than four decades building computer systems. He started on real-time hardware and software, including plant monitoring for nuclear power stations and an early digital radiography system. He then moved into finance, and as IT Director of the London International Financial Futures Exchange (LIFFE) he led the team behind APT, the first system to automate open-outcry trading.

Senior technology roles followed at Bankers Trust, Nikko Europe, Commerzbank and PwC, where he was Global Leader of Enterprise Core Systems. Today he is back close to the metal, writing MMBasic firmware for the Colour Maximite 2 and the PicoMite and designing the boards it runs on.
